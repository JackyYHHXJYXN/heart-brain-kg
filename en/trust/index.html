How we write

This is a public-information medical knowledge graph. It aims to help ordinary people understand, remember and use medical knowledge when they need it.

Where the content comes from

  • Compiled from published authoritative guidelines and institutional pages (for example the American Heart Association, the European Society of Cardiology, the UK NHS and Chinese guidelines).
  • Every topic lists its references. At the moment sources are given for the topic as a whole, not yet matched sentence by sentence to the original text. We are checking them one by one and will mark those that are done.
  • Every number on a page must be findable in the matching source; if it cannot be found, it is not published.
  • The content is compiled with AI assistance.

Review grades

  • Grade A: first-aid actions, medicines, whether and when to get care, and self-assessment tools. These need sign-off by a licensed doctor; pages without a signature say clearly “not yet signed off by a doctor”.
  • Grade B: understanding the condition, risk factors, tests and measurements, and lifestyle. Automatic checks plus independent review.
  • Scoring tools only add up points transparently, showing every item's score. They do not give a personal risk percentage.

What we do not do

  • We do not give personal medical advice and cannot replace a doctor.
  • Public pages do not give medicine doses or hospital quality targets; they only cover what readers can do themselves.
  • We accept no commercial funding, run no advertising and send no traffic to commercial services.

About the operator

The operator works in health-industry consulting. No personal name is published at this stage.

About the other language editions

  • The Traditional Chinese edition is converted automatically from the Simplified text (Taiwan usage); medical terms have not yet been proofread one by one. The English edition is written separately and has only been cross-checked by AI models from the same vendor, not by a doctor.

Privacy

No advertising, no cookies, no personal information collected; only anonymous aggregate statistics. Reading settings such as text size and colours are saved only on your device. When statistics go live, their purpose, provider and how to opt out will be stated here.

Text without line-by-line sources

Name equivalents, navigation and very basic explanations are not given a source one by one. Anything involving numbers, times, medicines, first aid or when to seek care always has a source, line by line. These texts are also on the review list, and any that raise doubt will be changed to carry a line-by-line source.
